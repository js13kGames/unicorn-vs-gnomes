## Description
Gnomes have stolen rainbow and now you have to restore it.

## Gameplay
Match colors to gather 30 of each.
Select chain of 3 or more orbs of the same color (axially and diagonally) to gather them.
Gnomes will try to steal colors you gathered.
You can attack them by spending colors noted under them.
To refresh board click `Refresh` button (cost 1 of each color).

Gradually stronger gnomes will appear to steal your rainbow.

Enemy types:
- Gnome - typical gnome: small, slow and clumsy
- Gnome thief - faster than normal gnome, steals colors from the board
- Gnome warrior - stronger and tougher than regular gnome
- Gnome knight - gnome elite warrior
- Gnome wizard - temporary freeze colors on board

## Controls
Mouse / touchscreen
You can choose between 2 methods to select and gather colors:
- **Mouse / touch release** - hold and drag mouse / finger on orbs to select them; release to gather. 
- **Match button** - click / tap orbs to select them; confirm selection with `Match` button.

Click / tap on gnomes to attack them.
